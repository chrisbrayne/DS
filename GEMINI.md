## Project Objective
Digital Scholar (DS) is a training and development initiative designed to empower AMS staff with the skills and applications necessary to support digital scholarship. This project directly contributes to the development of the AMS business by modernising research workflows, enhancing data management, and integrating advanced academic tools into consultancy delivery.

## Target Audience
*   **Primary:** AMS internal staff (Archaeologists, Consultants, and Researchers).
*   **Secondary:** Management (for strategic business development and capability mapping).

## Key Deliverables
*   **DS Resource Site:** A MkDocs-powered repository of information, tutorials, and reviews of digital scholarship tools.
*   **Tool Integrations:** Strategic implementation of external (e.g., Zotero) and internal tools.
*   **Training Modules:** Guidelines and workflows for digital research excellence.

## Directory Structure
*   `docs/`: Source files for the MkDocs site.
*   `mkdocs.yml`: Configuration for the DS resource site.
*   `research/`: In-depth analysis and benchmarking of DS tools.
*   `tutorials/`: Step-by-step guides for AMS staff.

## Usage
This module serves as the 'Learning & Development' hub for the AMS digital transition. It should be used to research new tools, document best practices, and host the internal knowledge base for digital scholarship.
