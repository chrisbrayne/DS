# Digital Scholar: Future Programme Proposals

This document outlines strategic initiatives and technical enhancements proposed for the expansion of the **AMS Digital Scholar (DS)** programme. These items are for management consideration to drive deeper adoption and professional excellence across the consultancy.

## 1. DS 'Lunch & Learn' Tactical Series
A series of 15-minute, high-impact briefings recorded for the DS site, focusing on specific "Technical Wins":
*   **Photo Efficiency:** "The 5-Minute Photo Register (Tropy to CSV)".
*   **Visual Phasing:** "Visualising Site Phasing in Obsidian Canvas".
*   **Literature Mining:** "Using ResearchRabbit to find Irish Grey Literature".

## 2. Standardised AMS Template Library
Development of downloadable configuration files to ensure company-wide consistency:
*   **Zotero CSL Styles:** Custom citation styles matching the AMS House Style for reports.
*   **Tropy Metadata Profiles:** Pre-configured Dublin Core templates for Irish/UK archaeology.
*   **Obsidian Vault Starters:** A "Site Starter" vault with pre-built Dataview queries for context and finds tracking.

## 3. Internal 'Digital Scholar' Accreditation
A three-tier recognition system to gamify adoption:
*   **DS Practitioner:** Basic setup (Zotero, browser extensions, AMS email linked).
*   **DS Specialist:** Demonstrated use of synthesis tools (e.g., shared NotebookLM on a live project).
*   **DS Fellow:** Contributing new tutorials or templates to the DS Resource Site.

## 4. Specialised AMS AI Agents
Development of internal agents trained on AMS SOPs and regional legislative requirements:
*   **EIAR Synthesis Agent:** Designed to scan TII reports for environmental mitigation trends.
*   **CRAV Preflight Agent:** Automated consistency checks for bibliographies and site codes.

## 5. Field-to-Scholar Mobile Integration
Guidance for bridging the gap between site excavation and digital research:
*   **Hypothesis Mobile:** Protocols for site-based digital annotation of planning documents.
*   **3D Finds Cataloguing:** Integrating Polycam/Lidar scans of finds directly into Obsidian site notes.

## 6. The 'Scholar-Consultant' Support Fund
Dedication of "Scholarship Hours" (e.g., 2 hours per month) for staff to use DS tools for converting grey literature into academic publications or public-facing "StoryMaps," enhancing both individual professional profiles and company reputation.
