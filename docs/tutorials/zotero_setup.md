# Tutorial: Setting up an AMS Zotero Group Library

This tutorial guides AMS Project Leads through the process of creating and managing a **Zotero Group Library**. Group Libraries are the cornerstone of the **Digital Scholar (DS)** programme, enabling real-time collaboration on site reports, academic papers, and grey literature.

## Prerequisites
*   A registered Zotero account (using your `@ams-archaeology.com` email).
*   Zotero Desktop installed on your machine.
*   Zotero Connector installed in your browser.

---

## Step 1: Create the Group
1.  Log in to [zotero.org](https://www.zotero.org/) in your browser.
2.  Navigate to the **Groups** tab in the top header.
3.  Click **"Create a New Group"**.
4.  **Group Name:** Use the standard AMS format: `AMS - [Project Name/Site Code]` (e.g., `AMS - Kildare-Meath Upgrade`).
5.  **Group Type:** Select **Private Membership**. This ensures that only invited AMS staff can see the library and its contents.

## Step 2: Configure Library Settings
Once the group is created, set the following 'Library Settings' to ensure maximum collaboration:
*   **Library Reading:** Any group member.
*   **Library Editing:** Any group member.
*   **File Editing:** Any group member.
*   **Action:** Click **"Save Settings"**.

## Step 3: Invite the Team
1.  Go to **Member Settings** within your new group.
2.  Click **"Send More Invitations"**.
3.  Enter the AMS email addresses of your project team.
4.  **Important:** Also invite the generic company account `ai@ams-archaeology.com` and set it to **'Admin'** status. This ensures the library remains accessible if an individual staff member leaves.

## Step 4: Sync to Desktop
1.  Open your **Zotero Desktop** application.
2.  Click the green 'Sync' icon in the top right corner.
3.  Your new Group Library will now appear in the left-hand pane under **"Group Libraries"**.

## Step 5: Establishing the "Inbox" Workflow
To keep the library professional and clean, create the following collection structure:
1.  Right-click on your Group Library and select **"New Collection"**.
2.  Name it `_INBOX`.
3.  Instruct your team to drop all new web-clipped sources into this folder first.
4.  The Project Lead will then verify the metadata (following the [CRAV protocol](../tools/zotero.md)) before moving the item to the permanent project folders (e.g., `Planning Documents`, `Comparative Sites`, `Specialist Reports`).

---

## Troubleshooting
*   **Files not syncing?** Ensure you have accepted the invitation in your email before attempting to sync.
*   **Metadata is messy?** Use the **"Right-click -> Retrieve Metadata for PDF"** feature, but always perform a manual check for Site Codes and Townland names.
