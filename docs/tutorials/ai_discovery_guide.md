# AI-Powered Discovery: Perplexity & Research Rabbit

This tutorial explores how to use AI tools to rapidly map the archaeological research landscape. We focus on **Perplexity** for synthesis and **Research Rabbit** for citation mapping.

## 1. Perplexity: The Research Engine
Perplexity provides direct answers with citations. It is excellent for legislative summaries and regional context.

### Workflow:
1.  **Ask:** "What are the archaeological requirements for peatland development in Ireland?"
2.  **Refine:** Use the "Pro" mode for a deeper dive into recent planning precedents.
3.  **Verify:** Always click the citations to confirm the source material before including it in a report.

## 2. Research Rabbit: Mapping Citations
Research Rabbit allows you to find "missing" papers by visualising how publications are linked.

### Workflow:
1.  **Seed:** Add 1-2 key papers on your topic (e.g., a specific period or site type).
2.  **Discover:** Click "Similar Work" to see a visual map of related publications.
3.  **Export:** Select relevant papers and export them to your **Zotero Group Library**.
