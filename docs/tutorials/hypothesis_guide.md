# Collaborative Annotation with Hypothesis

Hypothesis allows the AMS team to collaboratively annotate PDFs and web pages in real-time. This guide covers setting up your account and participating in private AMS research groups.

## 1. Getting Started
1.  **Sign Up:** Create an account at [web.hypothes.is](https://web.hypothes.is/) using your AMS email.
2.  **Install Extension:** Add the Hypothesis extension to your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox).
3.  **Log In:** Click the Hypothesis icon in your browser toolbar and log in.

## 2. Joining the AMS Private Group
To ensure client confidentiality, all project-related annotations MUST occur within private groups.
1.  Request the **AMS Internal Research Group** invite link from the AI Team Lead.
2.  Once joined, ensure you select the group from the dropdown menu in the Hypothesis sidebar before you start annotating.

## 3. Annotating a Document
*   **Highlight:** Select text on a webpage or PDF to highlight it.
*   **Annotate:** Select text and click the "Annotate" icon to add a comment.
*   **Tags:** Use tags like `#Mitigation`, `#Geophysics`, or the **Site ID** to make annotations searchable.

## 4. Best Practices for AMS Staff
*   **Professionalism:** Annotations are visible to the entire team. Use formal, professional language.
*   **Privacy:** Never annotate sensitive client documents in the "Public" layer.
*   **Linguistic Standard:** Use **Standard English (UK & Ireland)** in all comments.
