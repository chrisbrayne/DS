# AMS Digital Scholar

Welcome to the **AMS Digital Scholar (DS)** resource hub. This site is dedicated to the training and development of AMS staff in the field of digital scholarship.

## Our Mission
To bridge the gap between traditional archaeological research and modern digital workflows, ensuring AMS remains at the forefront of the consultancy sector.

## Current Focus
*   **Reference Management:** Implementing Zotero for robust citation and research organisation.
*   **Knowledge Graphs:** Exploring Obsidian and other networked-thought tools.
*   **Automated Synthesis:** Integrating RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) with academic libraries.

## Getting Started: The Learning Pathway
To begin your journey as an AMS Digital Scholar, we recommend following our **Research Pathways**, which guide you from initial discovery to final synthesis:

1.  **[Our Strategy](workflow_guide.md):** Understand the "Research Chain" and the Consultant-Scholar model.
2.  **[Phase 1: Discovery](tools/ai_discovery.md):** Map the research landscape using Perplexity and Research Rabbit.
3.  **[Phase 2: Collection](tools/zotero.md):** Build your digital library with Zotero and Tropy.
4.  **[Phase 3: Annotation](tools/hypothesis.md):** Peer-review and annotate evidence via Hypothesis.
5.  **[Phase 4: Synthesis](tools/notebooklm.md):** Generate grounded insights with NotebookLM and Obsidian.
