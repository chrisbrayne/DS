# Hypothesis: Collaborative Evidence Annotation

Hypothesis is a web-based tool that allows the AMS team to collaboratively annotate PDFs and web pages in real-time. It is the primary tool for the "Peer-Review" phase of our digital scholarship, enabling consultants to share insights directly on the source material.

## Why Hypothesis for AMS?

*   **Real-Time Collaboration:** Multiple consultants can highlight and comment on the same planning document or EIAR simultaneously.
*   **Public & Private Groups:** We use **Private AMS Groups** to ensure client confidentiality while still allowing for internal knowledge sharing.
*   **Traceable Insights:** Every highlight is attributed to a specific staff member, creating an audit trail of the research process.

## Key Workflows

### 1. Reviewing Planning Conditions
When a new project is launched, the Project Lead can share a link to the Planning Conditions. Team members can highlight critical requirements and tag them (e.g., `#Mitigation`, `#Geophysics`, `#Testing`).

### 2. Peer-Review of Grey Literature
When reading a site report from a previous excavation, consultants can use Hypothesis to flag inconsistencies or notable finds that may impact our current project.

### 3. Exporting Evidence
Annotations can be exported to Markdown and imported into Obsidian. This ensures that the insights gained during the reading phase are directly integrated into the writing phase.

## Best Practices
*   **Use Tags:** Always tag your annotations with the relevant **Site ID** or **Subject Matter**.
*   **Be Professional:** Remember that annotations in a Private Group are visible to all AMS team members. Use clear, formal language.
*   **Stick to Private Groups:** Ensure you are logged into the **AMS Internal Research Group** before annotating sensitive client documents.

## Setup Guide for AMS Staff
1.  **Sign Up:** Create an account at [web.hypothes.is](https://web.hypothes.is/) using your AMS email.
2.  **Extension:** Install the Hypothesis browser extension for Chrome or Edge.
3.  **Groups:** Join the **AMS Internal Research** group using the link provided in the [AI Strategy](../../AI%20Strategy/README.md) module.
4.  **Annotate:** Click the Hypothesis icon in your browser to start highlighting.
