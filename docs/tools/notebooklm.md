# Google NotebookLM: The Cognitive Operating System

Google NotebookLM is a foundational tool for the **AMS Digital Scholar (DS)** programme, serving as a "Cognitive Operating System" for complex archaeological research. It allows staff to upload a curated collection of sources—PDFs, website URLs, and YouTube transcripts—and use a grounded AI to synthesise, query, and transform that data into actionable insights.

## Why NotebookLM for AMS?

*   **Source Grounding:** Unlike general AI models, NotebookLM is strictly "grounded" in your uploaded sources. It will not cite information outside of your project files, virtually eliminating the risk of AI hallucinations.
*   **Multimedia Synthesis:** It can transform dense site reports into **Audio Overviews** (AI podcasts) or **Video Presentations**, making it an ideal tool for rapid internal briefings or client previews.
*   **Massive Context:** With a 1-million-token context window per source and support for up to 300 sources, it can ingest the entire "grey literature" history of a major landscape project in seconds.

## The "Scholar-Consultant" Workflow

### 1. Integration with Perplexity (Discovery to Synthesis)
AMS researchers use **Perplexity** to discover the latest web-based data and official site reports. Using the **Perplexity to NotebookLM** extension, these findings are exported directly into a focused notebook. This creates a seamless pipeline from real-time web search to deep, grounded analysis, bypassing the need for manual copy-pasting and ensuring every synthesis is backed by a verified URL.

### 2. Integration with Zotero (Evidence to Archive)
While NotebookLM handles the active synthesis, **Zotero** remains our primary archive. 

*   **PDF Sync:** By syncing your Zotero PDF storage to a Google Drive folder, NotebookLM can "watch" your library and automatically update its knowledge base as you add new sources. This ensures the AI is always querying the most recent version of your project literature.
*   **Insight Export:** Use the **NotebookLM to Zotero** extension to move AI-generated Q&As, summaries, and key citations back into your permanent Zotero project library for long-term reference and citation in final reports.

### 3. Collaborative Review
Project Leads can share a "Notebook" with their team. This allows multiple consultants to query the same set of evidence (e.g., "What are the common stratigraphic themes across all five test-trenching reports?") and share "Saved Notes" of key AI insights.

## Setup Guide for AMS Staff
1.  **Access:** Log in to [notebooklm.google.com](https://notebooklm.google.com/) using your AMS corporate account.
2.  **Create:** Start a new notebook named after your **Site ID** or **Project Name**.
3.  **Sources:** Upload relevant EIARs, NMS records, or TII reports.
4.  **Extensions:** Ensure the *Perplexity to NotebookLM* and *NotebookLM to Zotero* extensions are active in your browser.
