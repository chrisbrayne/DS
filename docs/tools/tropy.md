# Tropy: Managing Archaeological Imagery

Tropy is an open-source tool designed specifically for researchers to organise and describe large collections of digital photos. For AMS, Tropy is the essential link between raw field photography (site photos, sections, finds) and the structured metadata required for professional archive deposition.

## Why Tropy for AMS?

*   **Standardised Metadata:** Tropy allows us to attach **Dublin Core** or custom archaeological metadata to every photo (e.g., *Site Code*, *Context Number*, *Direction of View*).
*   **Bulk Editing:** Quickly update metadata for hundreds of site photos at once, saving hours of manual data entry.
*   **Transcription & Notes:** Add detailed transcriptions of site notebooks or field sketches directly to the associated image.

## Key Features for Fieldwork

### 1. Custom Metadata Templates
AMS staff should use the **'AMS Archaeology Template'** in Tropy. This includes fields for:
*   **Site Code:** The unique project identifier.
*   **Context/Feature:** The specific stratigraphic unit.
*   **Subject:** (e.g., Section, Plan, Post-excavation find).
*   **Photographer:** To ensure proper attribution in final reports.

### 2. Photo Organisation
Tropy does not move your files; it creates a database that points to them. You can group photos into 'Items' (e.g., all photos of a single burial) and 'Lists' (e.g., all photos from Week 1 of an excavation).

### 3. Export for Reporting
Once your photos are described, you can export the metadata as a CSV or JSON file. This can be used to automatically generate the "Photo Register" appendix in your final report.

## Integration with the AMS Ecosystem
*   **Obsidian:** Link to specific Tropy items or exported images from your site notes.
*   **Final Reports:** Use exported CSV data to populate the photo tables in Word using the **AMS Document Factory**.

## Setup Guide for AMS Staff
1.  **Download:** Install Tropy from [tropy.org](https://tropy.org/).
2.  **Project:** Create a new Tropy project for your current site.
3.  **Template:** Go to *Settings* -> *Templates* and import the AMS Metadata Standard.
4.  **Import:** Drag and drop your site photos to begin describing them.
