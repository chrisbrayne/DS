# AI Discovery: Perplexity & Research Rabbit

Artificial Intelligence discovery tools are revolutionising how we map the research landscape for archaeological consultancy. For the **AMS Digital Scholar (DS)** programme, we utilise **Perplexity** for rapid synthesis and **Research Rabbit** for visualising citation networks.

## Perplexity: The Research Engine

Perplexity is an AI-powered search engine that provides direct answers to complex questions, backed by real-world citations. It is used at AMS for:
*   **Legislative Summaries:** "What are the current requirements for underwater archaeology under the Irish National Monuments Act?"
*   **Regional Context:** "Summarise the Mesolithic archaeology of the Shannon estuary based on recent TII reports."
*   **Rapid Fact-Checking:** Verifying site names, dates, and historical figures during the drafting process.

### Best Practices for Perplexity
*   **Verify Sources:** Always click the citations to ensure the AI hasn't "hallucinated" or misinterpreted a source.
*   **Use "Pro" Mode:** For complex archaeological queries, use the 'Pro' or 'Writing' modes for deeper analysis.
*   **UK/Ireland Focus:** Explicitly ask for results focused on UK and Irish standards to avoid US-centric legal drift.

## Research Rabbit: Mapping the Network

Research Rabbit is a "Spotify for Papers" that allows you to visually map how academic papers and reports are connected.

### Key Workflows
*   **Seed Papers:** Add a key publication (e.g., a foundational paper on Irish fulachtaí fia) to a collection.
*   **Visualise:** Research Rabbit will show you a "Map" of every paper that has cited that source, and every source that paper cited.
*   **Discover:** It suggests "Similar Work" and "Early/Later Work," helping you find obscure but relevant reports that might have been missed in a standard keyword search.

## Integration with Zotero
Both tools allow for easy export to Zotero. Once you discover a relevant source in Research Rabbit, you can save it to your Zotero Group Library with one click.
